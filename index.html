Services

Software that watches the numbers you don't have time to.

Each of these connects directly to your Seller Central account through the SP API, no manual exports, no spreadsheets to babysit.

01

Real time profit tracking

Profit calculated as orders come in, factoring in cost of goods, Amazon fees, and ad spend, broken down at the SKU level so you can see exactly which products are making money and which are quietly losing it.

02

Buy Box & competitor tracking

Continuous monitoring of Buy Box status alongside competitor pricing, so a loss shows up as an alert with context, not something you notice a week later.

03

Automated reimbursement case creation

Lost or damaged FBA inventory is detected and matched against orders automatically, with reimbursement cases created for you instead of filed by hand.

04

Fee change alerts

Amazon adjusts referral and other fees without much notice. Changes are tracked and surfaced automatically, so a shift in fees doesn't quietly eat your margin.

05

Automated invoice generation

Invoices created automatically by pulling straight from your order data, no manual entry, no reconciling against Seller Central by hand.
